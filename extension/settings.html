The model menu in the Tutor tab's header lists these, so you can switch with one click while you work. The default is what the tutor uses now; the provider settings above (server, key, where it runs) apply to every model of that provider. Changes take effect when you save.

Checking

The Claude and ChatGPT subscriptions run through a small helper app on your computer, which needs Node. Once it is installed, the extension starts the tutor when you open a LeetCode problem and stops it 30 seconds after you close the last one.
